Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

The short version: Wildlands Rally does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no sign-in, no user accounts and no server of our own. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information about you.

What the game keeps on your device

Wildlands Rally saves your progress locally so you can pick up where you left off. That includes the profile name you choose, your coins, unlocked vehicles, upgrade levels, colour and marking choices, your record card, and settings such as volume and difficulty.

All of it stays on your device. We have no way to see it.

Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup on iPhone or iPad, or Auto Backup on Android, your device may include the game’s saved data in your own backup, stored under your own Apple or Google account. That is a feature of your device and is governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy, not ours. We have no access to it.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. The game contains no advertising, no analytics or measurement tools, no tracking technologies, and no third-party components that gather information about you.

Internet access

Wildlands Rally plays entirely offline. It does not need an internet connection and does not send data anywhere while you play.

Children’s privacy

The game is made to be suitable for children as well as adults. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 — knowingly or otherwise. There is nothing on our side for a parent to review, correct or ask us to delete, because we hold nothing at all. This is intended to satisfy the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent rules elsewhere.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete. Removing the app from your device deletes the saved data stored there.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game collects anything — for example if online play or cloud saves are added — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the privacy information shown on the App Store and Google Play will be updated at the same time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@wildlandsrally.com
