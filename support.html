Support

We read every message and aim to reply within a few days.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@wildlandsrally.com. If you are reporting a problem, please include your device, its iOS or Android version, and the build number shown on the main menu.

Frequently asked

How do I save my progress?
It saves itself. Everything you earn is stored against the player you are using, on your device, as you go.
Can more than one person play on the same device?
Yes. Tap Players at the top of the screen and add one each. Every player keeps their own coins, garage and record card. There is no sign-in and nothing to set up beyond a name.
Will my progress move to a new phone or tablet?
If iCloud Backup or Android Auto Backup is switched on, your saved data travels with your device backup under your own Apple or Google account. It is not stored by us, so we cannot restore it for you.
I deleted the app and lost everything.
Deleting the app removes its saved data from the device. If you had a device backup from before, restoring it brings the progress back.
How do I delete a player, or all my data?
Open Players and use Delete on any player you no longer want; it takes two taps to confirm. To remove everything, delete the app — that is all of it, because nothing is stored anywhere but the device.
Where are the sound and music controls?
In Settings on the profile page, and also from the pause menu during a match. Music and effects have separate sliders.
Can I play against other people?
Not yet — you race nine computer-controlled rivals. Playing against real people is something we are looking at for a future update.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. The whole game runs offline.
The game feels slow on my device.
Wildlands Rally quietly lowers its rendering resolution if a device cannot hold a steady frame rate. If it is still struggling, closing other apps usually helps.
